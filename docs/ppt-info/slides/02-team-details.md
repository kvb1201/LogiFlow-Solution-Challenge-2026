## Problem statement (paste as one paragraph)

India moves billions of tonnes of freight yearly, yet most shippers plan in **a single transport mode** — road only, or rail only — without comparing cost, time, and risk across alternatives. Small businesses and student logistics projects lack access to **unified multimodal planning** with real schedules, tariffs, traffic, and delay intelligence. The result is higher cost, missed deadlines, and blind risk on corridors where rail or water may be clearly better.
